Today's speaker is Ed Susko.

Ed Susko received his PhD in 1996 from the University of Waterloo, working under the supervision of Jack Kalbfleisch and Jiahua Chen. After a postdoctoral fellowship at the University of Toronto, he joined the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Dalhousie University in 1998. While there he became part of a larger group working on evolutionary questions of various sorts with some emphasis on deep divergences. Statistical issues in molecular evolution remain a focus of his research. He has worked on developing statistical tests, model selection, adaptive models and models for process heterogeneity.

Welcome, Ed, and thank you for participating!
